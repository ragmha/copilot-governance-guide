This independent course is for enterprise and platform administrators working with security, finance and engineering stakeholders. Each part introduces a governance decision, illustrates it with hypothetical examples, and ends with questions to discuss.

Each part's summary has a scored quiz. Answer every question correctly to unlock the next part; you can retry without penalty. Progress is stored in this browser, not in an account. JavaScript and browser storage are required for progression. The quiz is a learning aid, not a certification or tamper-proof assessment.

The examples do not change production settings. Confirm current documentation, your plan, permissions and your organization's approval process before acting. Advice is labeled as such; product behavior is linked to GitHub's sources.

The course adapts ideas from [Copilot governance at scale](https://github.com/octodemo/copilot-governance-at-scale); its source content and this guide require human review. See the [sources and status register](/sources/) for review boundaries.
